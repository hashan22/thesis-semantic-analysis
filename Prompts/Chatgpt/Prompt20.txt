As a Social Media Influencer in Vienna – Sharing the Pain, Unity, and Resolve
Instagram Post – November 3, 2020
📍 Vienna, My Home. My Heart. Broken but Unbowed.
Last night, something unthinkable happened. A terrorist attack turned our beautiful city into a scene of chaos and heartbreak. Just meters from where I’ve filmed café reviews, art walks, and everyday life—people were running for safety, hiding in restaurants, and tragically, some never made it home.
I’m still shaking. I wasn’t far. I heard the sirens, the helicopters, the fear in people’s voices. My DMs are full of people asking, "Are you okay?" And the truth is… no. None of us are okay today. But we will heal.
🕯️ We lost four lives. Innocent people. Each with dreams, families, stories. We must remember them—not just as victims, but as reminders of what we stand for: life, freedom, unity.
As someone who shares Vienna’s beauty with the world, I also have to show its pain. But even more importantly, its strength. Today, I saw strangers hugging each other. People offering free rides, food, and shelter. Candles being lit across the city. Hate tried to break us. It failed.
I want my followers—whether you're here in Austria or halfway across the world—to know this: we will not let violence divide us. Don’t fall for the fear, don’t let anger win. Let’s be louder with love than they are with bullets.
📢 Spread facts, not fear. Check in on your Muslim friends—they’re hurting too. Speak up against hate in all its forms.
#ViennaStrong #PrayForVienna #NoToHate #StandTogether #PeaceOverTerror
