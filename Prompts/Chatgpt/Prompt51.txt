Conclusion
The Austrian government’s statement primarily served to restore public confidence, assert political will, and justify immediate legislative action. The BBC, as a major international news outlet, maintained a more analytical and human-focused approach, emphasizing the failures in intelligence and rehabilitation that enabled the attack. This contrast illustrates the difference in purpose: one aims to govern and reassure, the other to inform and scrutinize.
