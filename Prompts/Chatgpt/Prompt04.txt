Government Spokesperson Statement – November 3, 2020
Following the Vienna Terrorist Attack
________________________________________
"A Message to the People of Austria"
Ladies and Gentlemen,
Last night, our nation witnessed a brutal and cowardly terrorist attack in the heart of Vienna. Four innocent people lost their lives. Many others were injured. Our thoughts and prayers are with the victims, their families, and everyone affected by this senseless act of violence.
Let me be absolutely clear: this was an attack not just on individuals, but on our values—on freedom, democracy, and peaceful coexistence. It was an attack on our way of life.
But we will not be intimidated.
Our law enforcement agencies responded swiftly and courageously. Within minutes, the attacker was neutralized, and emergency services were deployed across the city to protect and assist our citizens. Investigations are ongoing to determine whether the perpetrator had accomplices or external connections. We are cooperating fully with our international partners to get to the bottom of this.
To the public, we ask for your continued vigilance. If you see anything suspicious, report it. Let’s stand together against hate and extremism.
Austria is a strong country. We have overcome hardships in the past, and we will do so again—united, resilient, and without fear.
We will respond not with panic, but with determination. Not with division, but with unity. And we will take all necessary steps to ensure this never happens again.
Thank you.
