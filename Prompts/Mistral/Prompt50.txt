Key Themes and Strategies

1. Victimization and Grievance:
	 Extremist groups use narratives of victimization and grievance to foster a sense of shared identity, purpose, and solidarity among their supporters, as well as to justify their use of violence and terrorism as a means to address these perceived injustices.
	 This narrative emphasizes the supposed oppression, discrimination, and marginalization of their community, as well as the need for resistance, retaliation, and liberation.
2. Clash of Civilizations:
	 Extremist groups promote a narrative of an inevitable and existential clash between civilizations, cultures, or religions to create a sense of urgency, fear, and polarization, as well as to justify their use of violence and terrorism as a means to defend their community and advance their ideology.
	 This narrative emphasizes the supposed incompatibility, conflict, and confrontation between these groups, as well as the need for separation, domination, and victory.
3. Martyrdom and Heroism:
	 Extremist groups glorify and celebrate individuals who carry out attacks as martyrs and heroes to create a sense of admiration, emulation, and competition among their supporters, as well as to justify and encourage the use of violence and terrorism as a means to achieve their goals.
	 This narrative emphasizes the supposed courage, sacrifice, and honor of these individuals, as well as the need for their supporters to follow in their footsteps and engage in violent extremism.
4. Divine Sanction and Justification:
	 Extremist groups invoke religious or ideological justifications for their actions to create a sense of legitimacy, authority, and obligation among their supporters, as well as to justify and encourage the use of violence and terrorism as a means to fulfill their religious or ideological duties.
	 This narrative emphasizes the supposed divine sanction, approval, or command for their use of violence and terrorism, as well as the need for their supporters to obey and implement these supposed mandates.
5. Conspiracy and Deception:
	 Extremist groups promote conspiracy theories and alternative narratives to create a sense of skepticism, distrust, and alienation among their supporters, as well as to justify and encourage the use of violence and terrorism as a means to expose and resist the supposed deception and manipulation of the political establishment.
	 This narrative emphasizes the supposed secrecy, conspiracy, and deception of the political establishment, as well as the need for their supporters to uncover and challenge these supposed truths.
6. Utopian Vision and Promise:
	 Extremist groups promote a utopian vision of the future to create a sense of hope, inspiration, and commitment among their supporters, as well as to justify and encourage the use of violence and terrorism as a means to achieve their ideological goals and build a better world.
	 This narrative emphasizes the supposed inevitability, desirability, and attainability of their utopian vision, as well as the need for their supporters to work and sacrifice to realize this supposed destiny.
7. Unity and Solidarity:
	 Extremist groups emphasize the importance of unity and solidarity among their supporters to create a sense of connection, loyalty, and cooperation, as well as to justify and encourage the use of violence and terrorism as a means to protect and promote their shared interests and values.
	 This narrative emphasizes the supposed strength, unity, and solidarity of their community, as well as the need for their supporters to stand together and engage in violent extremism to defend and advance their shared cause.

Conclusion

Extremist groups promote specific narratives about events like the Vienna attack to further their ideologies, recruit new members, and justify their actions. These narratives include themes of victimization and grievance, clash of civilizations, martyrdom and heroism, divine sanction and justification, conspiracy and deception, utopian vision and promise, and unity and solidarity. Understanding these narratives and their purposes is essential for developing effective counterterrorism strategies that address the root causes of extremism, promote social cohesion and resilience, and foster a more peaceful, secure, and inclusive society. By learning from these narratives and their implications, Austria and the international community can work together to prevent similar incidents and build a brighter future for all. This includes investing in community engagement and resilience programs, educational initiatives, and support for grassroots organizations that promote dialogue, understanding, and cooperation among diverse communities, as well as enhancing partnerships and collaboration with international counterparts to combat extremism and terrorism and promote a more peaceful and secure world.
