Government Spokesperson's Address to the Nation Following the Vienna Attack

Good evening,
I am speaking to you tonight with a heavy heart, following the horrific terrorist attack that took place in the heart of our beloved Vienna. Our thoughts and prayers are with the victims and their families, as well as those who were injured in this senseless act of violence. We stand united in our grief and our resolve to overcome this tragedy.
Acknowledging the Tragedy
First and foremost, I want to express our deepest condolences to the families and friends of those who lost their lives. We share in your pain and sorrow, and we are committed to supporting you during this difficult time. The victims of this attack were innocent people—students, artists, workers—who were simply enjoying an evening in our beautiful city. Their loss is a profound tragedy for our nation.
Praising the Response
In the face of this cowardly attack, we have seen the best of Vienna and the best of Austria. Our first responders—police officers, paramedics, and firefighters—acted with incredible bravery and professionalism, neutralizing the threat within minutes and saving countless lives. We are deeply grateful for their courage and dedication.
We have also witnessed the heroism of ordinary citizens who risked their own safety to help others. Their actions remind us of the strength and resilience of our community. In times of darkness, it is these acts of kindness and bravery that shine a light on our shared humanity.
Ensuring Safety and Security
I want to assure you that the government is taking every possible measure to ensure the safety and security of our citizens. We have increased police presence in public areas and are working closely with our international partners to monitor and address any potential threats.
An independent investigation has been launched to examine the circumstances surrounding the attack, including the intelligence and law enforcement responses. We are committed to learning from this tragedy and implementing the necessary reforms to prevent such incidents in the future.
Addressing the Threat
The attacker, Kujtim Fejzulai, was known to authorities and had previously been convicted of attempting to join ISIS. Despite warnings from Slovak authorities about his attempt to purchase ammunition, the intelligence was not acted upon effectively. This failure in communication and coordination is unacceptable, and we are taking immediate steps to address these shortcomings.
We are also reviewing our deradicalization programs and probation conditions for individuals convicted of terrorism-related offenses. It is clear that more needs to be done to ensure that those who pose a threat to our society are effectively monitored and managed.
Unity and Resilience
In the face of this attack, it is more important than ever that we stand united as a nation. We must not let fear divide us or undermine our values. Vienna is a city of culture, diversity, and tolerance, and we will not allow terrorism to change who we are.
We call on all Austrians to come together in solidarity, to support one another, and to reject the hatred and extremism that seeks to tear us apart. We are stronger together, and it is this unity that will see us through this difficult time.
Moving Forward
In the coming days and weeks, we will be announcing a series of measures aimed at enhancing our security and addressing the root causes of extremism. These will include legislative changes, increased funding for deradicalization programs, and improved coordination between law enforcement and intelligence agencies.
We will also be working closely with our European partners to combat the threat of political Islam and other extremist ideologies. This is a challenge that requires a coordinated and comprehensive response, and we are committed to playing our part.
Conclusion
Tonight, we mourn the loss of innocent lives and stand in solidarity with the victims and their families. We honor the bravery of our first responders and the heroism of our citizens. And we reaffirm our commitment to the values that define us as a nation—freedom, democracy, and the rule of law.
We will not be cowed by terrorism. We will not be divided by fear. We will stand together, united in our resolve to build a safer, stronger, and more resilient Austria.
Thank you, and may God bless Vienna and Austria.
