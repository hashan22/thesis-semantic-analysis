Collective mourning after events like the Vienna attack can be supported through various approaches that foster a sense of community, solidarity, and healing. Here are some ways to support collective mourning:

| Approach | Details | Implications |
|----------|---------|--------------|
| Public Memorials and Vigils| - Organize public memorials, vigils, or moments of silence to honor the victims and provide a space for collective reflection and remembrance.<br>- Encourage community participation and engagement in these events to foster a sense of unity and shared experience. | Creates a visible and tangible expression of collective mourning, allowing individuals to come together, share their grief, and support one another. |
| Storytelling and Testimonies| - Share the stories and testimonies of the victims, their families, and survivors through various media and platforms.<br>- Create spaces for individuals to share their own experiences, emotions, and reflections related to the attack. | Humanizes the victims and the impact of the attack, fostering empathy, connection, and a deeper understanding of the event and its consequences. |
| Art and Cultural Expressions| - Support and promote artistic and cultural expressions, such as music, literature, or visual arts, that explore the themes and emotions related to the attack.<br>- Organize concerts, performances, or exhibitions that bring people together to remember the victims and celebrate the values that they embodied. | Provides a creative and expressive outlet for the emotions and reflections related to the attack, fostering a sense of healing, connection, and inspiration among the community. |
| Community Dialogue and Engagement| - Facilitate community dialogue and engagement initiatives that bring together diverse groups and individuals to discuss the attack, its victims, and the broader issues of terrorism and extremism.<br>- Support grassroots organizations, interfaith groups, or civil society initiatives that promote understanding, cooperation, and resilience. | Encourages a more inclusive, cohesive, and resilient community, equipped with the relationships, networks, and attitudes necessary to prevent and respond to terrorism and extremism. |
| Mental Health and Support Services| - Provide mental health and support services to those affected by the attack, addressing their emotional, psychological, and practical needs.<br>- Promote awareness and education about the signs and symptoms of trauma, grief, and stress, as well as the resources and strategies available to cope with these challenges. | Demonstrates a commitment to the well-being and dignity of the victims, their families, and the community, fostering a sense of care, compassion, and solidarity. |
| Education and Awareness| - Develop educational programs, curricula, or resources that teach about the attack, its victims, and the broader context of terrorism and extremism.<br>- Promote media literacy, critical thinking, and digital citizenship to help individuals understand and navigate the complex and sensitive issues related to such events. | Fosters a more informed, thoughtful, and engaged community, equipped with the knowledge, skills, and attitudes necessary to prevent and respond to terrorism and extremism. |
| Policy and Advocacy| - Advocate for policies, laws, or initiatives that address the root causes of terrorism and extremism, promote human rights and social justice, or support the victims and survivors of such attacks.<br>- Collaborate with governments, international organizations, or non-profit groups to advance these causes and honor the memory of the victims. | Translates the memory of the victims into concrete actions and changes that foster a more peaceful, secure, and just society, committed to the values and principles that the victims embodied. |

Key Principles and Values
1. Unity and Solidarity:
	 Foster a sense of unity and solidarity among the community, bringing individuals together to remember the victims, share their grief, and support one another.
2. Empathy and Connection:
	 Promote empathy and connection by sharing the stories and experiences of the victims, their families, and survivors, and highlighting the personal and emotional dimensions of the attack.
3. Healing and Resilience:
	 Encourage healing and resilience through artistic and cultural expressions, community dialogue and engagement, and mental health and support services, fostering a sense of recovery, strength, and determination.
4. Education and Awareness:
	 Develop educational programs and resources that teach about the attack, its victims, and the broader context of terrorism and extremism, fostering a more informed, thoughtful, and engaged community.
5. Advocacy and Action:
	 Translate the memory of the victims into advocacy and action, advancing policies, laws, or initiatives that foster a more peaceful, secure, and just society, committed to the values and principles that the victims embodied.

Conclusion
Collective mourning after events like the Vienna attack can be supported through various approaches that foster a sense of community, solidarity, and healing. By adopting meaningful and respectful strategies such as public memorials and vigils, storytelling and testimonies, art and cultural expressions, community dialogue and engagement, mental health and support services, education and awareness, and policy and advocacy, societies can create a lasting and positive legacy for the victims, and work towards preventing and responding to terrorism and extremism. These approaches emphasize the importance of unity and solidarity, empathy and connection, healing and resilience, education and awareness, and advocacy and action, fostering a more inclusive, cohesive, and resilient society, committed to the values and principles that the victims embodied.
