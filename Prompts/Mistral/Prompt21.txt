Initial Reports and Confusion
In the chaotic hours following the attack, there was significant confusion and misinformation. Initial reports suggested the possibility of multiple attackers, which later turned out to be incorrect. The rapid response by Austrian police helped to neutralize the threat quickly, but the initial uncertainty added to the fear and anxiety in the city.

Authorities' Response
Austrian authorities, including the police and government officials, provided regular updates to the public as more information became available. They urged caution and asked people to avoid the city center while the situation was being brought under control.
