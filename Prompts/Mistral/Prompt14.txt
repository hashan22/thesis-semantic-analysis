EU Security Advisor's Statement on the Vienna Terrorist Attack
Brussels, November 3, 2020
Ladies and Gentlemen,
I stand before you today with a heavy heart following the horrific terrorist attack that took place in the heart of Vienna last night. On behalf of the European Union, I extend our deepest condolences to the families and friends of the victims, and our unwavering support to the Austrian people and government. This cowardly act of violence is an assault on our shared European values of freedom, democracy, and human dignity.
Assessment of the Attack
The attack, carried out by a lone gunman with known extremist ties, has once again highlighted the persistent and evolving threat of terrorism that our continent faces. The perpetrator, Kujtim Fejzulai, was a known extremist with a history of radicalization and attempts to join ISIS. Despite this, he was able to evade effective monitoring and intervention, and carry out this horrific act.
The attack has exposed critical vulnerabilities in our collective security apparatus, particularly in the areas of intelligence sharing, deradicalization programs, and the monitoring of known extremists. It is clear that we must redouble our efforts to address these shortcomings and enhance our counterterrorism capabilities.
EU's Response and Support
In the wake of this tragedy, the European Union stands ready to provide any and all necessary support to Austria. We have already activated the EU Crisis Response Mechanism, which will facilitate the deployment of resources and expertise to assist in the investigation and response efforts.
We are also in close contact with our Austrian counterparts, offering our full support and cooperation in the ongoing investigation. The EU's counterterrorism agencies, including Europol and the European Counter Terrorism Centre, are working tirelessly to provide intelligence and analytical support to the Austrian authorities.
Lessons Learned and the Way Forward
The Vienna attack serves as a stark reminder of the need for a comprehensive, coordinated, and proactive approach to counterterrorism. In light of this tragedy, I would like to highlight several key areas that require our immediate attention and action:
1. Enhancing Intelligence Sharing and Cooperation
The attack has underscored the critical importance of effective intelligence sharing and cooperation among EU member states. The fact that Slovak authorities had warned their Austrian counterparts about the perpetrator's attempt to purchase ammunition, but this intelligence was not acted upon effectively, is deeply concerning.
We must prioritize the development of robust intelligence-sharing mechanisms, both at the national and EU levels. This includes the establishment of real-time data sharing platforms, the enhancement of analytical capabilities, and the strengthening of trust and cooperation among our intelligence agencies.
2. Strengthening Deradicalization and Reintegration Programs
The Vienna attack has also highlighted the challenges and limitations of deradicalization and reintegration programs for convicted terrorists. The perpetrator had participated in such programs but was still able to carry out this horrific act.
We must work to enhance the effectiveness of these programs, ensuring that they are comprehensive, evidence-based, and tailored to the individual needs of participants. This includes providing ongoing support and monitoring, as well as addressing the underlying factors that contribute to radicalization and violent extremism.
3. Improving the Monitoring and Management of Known Extremists
The attack has raised serious questions about the monitoring and management of known extremists, particularly those who have been released from prison. We must review and strengthen our approaches to the supervision and control of such individuals, ensuring that they are effectively monitored and that any signs of re-radicalization or violent intent are swiftly addressed.
This may include the use of electronic monitoring, regular check-ins with law enforcement, and the imposition of strict probation conditions. It may also require the development of new legislative and policy frameworks to enhance our ability to manage and control high-risk individuals.
4. Addressing the Root Causes of Radicalization and Extremism
Ultimately, our efforts to counter terrorism must also address the root causes of radicalization and extremism. This includes tackling the social, economic, and political grievances that can fuel extremist ideologies, as well as promoting inclusion, tolerance, and understanding among our diverse communities.
We must work to foster strong and resilient societies, where all individuals feel valued, respected, and empowered to contribute to our shared European project. This includes investing in education, dialogue, and community engagement initiatives that promote social cohesion and challenge extremist narratives.
5. Enhancing EU-Wide Counterterrorism Capabilities
Finally, the Vienna attack underscores the need for a comprehensive and coordinated EU-wide approach to counterterrorism. We must continue to strengthen our collective capabilities, including through the enhancement of Europol's mandate and resources, the development of joint counterterrorism strategies, and the promotion of best practices and lessons learned among member states.
We must also work to enhance our external cooperation with key partners, including the United States, the United Kingdom, and other like-minded countries, to address the global nature of the terrorist threat.
Conclusion
The terrorist attack in Vienna is a tragic reminder of the persistent and evolving threat of terrorism that our continent faces. However, it is also a testament to the strength, resilience, and unity of the European people and our shared commitment to the values that define us.
In the face of this horrific act, we must stand together, united in our determination to confront and overcome the challenge of terrorism. We must redouble our efforts to enhance our collective security, address the root causes of radicalization and extremism, and promote a more peaceful, inclusive, and resilient Europe.
The European Union stands firmly with Austria in this dark hour, and we will continue to provide our full support and cooperation in the investigation and response efforts. Together, we will honor the memory of the victims, support the injured and their families, and work tirelessly to prevent such tragedies from occurring again.
Thank you.
[Your Name], EU Security Advisor
This statement is dedicated to the victims of the Vienna attack, their families, and the brave first responders who risked their lives to save others.
