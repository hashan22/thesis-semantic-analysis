In the immediate aftermath of the Vienna attack on November 2, 2020, the Austrian government took several swift and decisive actions to address the crisis, ensure public safety, and initiate investigations. Here are the key immediate government responses: 

| Response | Details |
|----------|---------|	
| Emergency Deployment| Rapid deployment of police, emergency medical services, and other first responders to the scene of the attack to neutralize the threat, tend to the injured, and secure the area. |
| Public Safety Measures | Implementation of a city-wide lockdown, with increased police presence in public areas, road closures, and public transportation disruptions to prevent further attacks and facilitate the investigation. |
| Communication and Updates | Regular press conferences and updates from government officials, including Chancellor Sebastian Kurz and Interior Minister Karl Nehammer, to inform the public about the situation, provide safety instructions, and address concerns. |
| Declaration of National Mourning| Announcement of a period of national mourning to honor the victims of the attack and express solidarity with their families and the injured. |
| Independent Investigation| Launch of an independent investigation into the intelligence failures and security lapses that allowed the attack to occur, with a focus on improving inter-agency coordination and communication. |
| Enhanced Security Measures| Announcement of plans to enhance national security, including the ability to keep individuals convicted of terror offenses behind bars for life through indefinite detention and electronic surveillance of released offenders. |
| International Cooperation| Coordination with international partners, including the European Union and other countries, to share intelligence, enhance counterterrorism efforts, and address the global threat of terrorism. |
| Support for Victims and Families| Provision of resources and support for the victims of the attack and their families, including financial assistance, counseling services, and other forms of aid. |
| Community Engagement| Efforts to engage with local communities, promote unity and resilience, and combat the spread of fear, division, and misinformation in the wake of the attack. |

Key Statements and Actions
1.	Chancellor Sebastian Kurz:
	 Held a press conference in the early hours of November 3, 2020, to address the nation and provide an update on the situation.
	 Expressed condolences to the victims and their families, praised the bravery of first responders, and vowed to take decisive action to enhance national security.
	 Announced plans to introduce legislation allowing for the indefinite detention of individuals convicted of terror offenses and electronic surveillance of released offenders.
2.	Interior Minister Karl Nehammer:
	 Provided regular updates on the investigation, the security situation, and the government's response to the attack.
	 Acknowledged the intelligence failures that allowed the attack to occur and announced an independent investigation to examine the circumstances surrounding the incident.
	 Emphasized the need for improved inter-agency coordination and communication to prevent future attacks.
3.	Vienna Mayor Michael Ludwig:
	 Addressed the city of Vienna, expressing solidarity with the victims and their families, and praising the bravery and resilience of the city's residents.
	 Announced a series of community events and initiatives to promote healing, unity, and support in the wake of the attack.

Conclusion
The Austrian government's immediate response to the Vienna attack was characterized by swift and decisive action to address the crisis, ensure public safety, and initiate investigations. The government's focus on enhancing national security, improving intelligence-sharing mechanisms, and supporting the victims and their families reflected a commitment to learning from the attack and preventing similar incidents in the future. The coordinated efforts of government officials, security agencies, and community leaders helped to restore a sense of calm and resilience in the wake of the tragedy.
