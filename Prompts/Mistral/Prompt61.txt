Societies should remember the victims of terrorist attacks like the Vienna 2020 incident in ways that honor their lives, support their families, and foster resilience, unity, and a commitment to the values that terrorists seek to undermine. | Art and Culture| - Commission or support artistic and cultural expressions, such as music, literature, or visual arts, that honor the victims and explore the themes and emotions related to the attack.<br>- Organize concerts, performances, or exhibitions that bring people together to remember the victims and celebrate the values that they embodied. | Provides a creative and expressive outlet for the emotions and reflections related to the attack, fostering a sense of healing, connection, and inspiration among society. |
