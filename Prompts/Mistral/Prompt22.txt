In the week following the Vienna attack on November 2, 2020, public opinion in Austria and beyond underwent significant shifts as more information about the attack and its perpetrator emerged. Here is an overview of how public sentiment evolved during that time:

| Aspect | Initial Reaction | Shift in Public Opinion | Factors Influencing the Shift |
|--------|------------------|------------------------|-----------------------------|
| Security Concerns| Shock and fear about the immediate threat and potential for further attacks. | Increased demand for stronger security measures and more effective counterterrorism strategies. | Revelations about the attacker's background and the intelligence failures that allowed the attack to occur. |
| Government Response| Initial support for the government's handling of the crisis and the rapid police response. | Growing criticism of the government's counterterrorism policies and intelligence-sharing mechanisms. | Reports of prior warnings about the attacker and questions about the effectiveness of deradicalization programs. |
| Immigration and Integration| Concerns about immigration and the integration of foreign-born citizens. | Heightened debates about immigration policies, with some calling for stricter controls and others advocating for better integration efforts. | The attacker's background as a second-generation immigrant and his history of radicalization. |
| Islam and Extremism | Fear and anxiety about the threat of Islamist extremism. | Increased scrutiny of Muslim communities and debates about the role of Islam in Austrian society. | The attacker's known extremist ties and the Islamic State's claim of responsibility for the attack. |
| Social Cohesion | A sense of unity and solidarity in the immediate aftermath of the attack. | Growing divisions and debates about the values and identity of Austrian society. | Political rhetoric and media coverage that emphasized cultural and religious differences. |
| Media Coverage | Initial focus on the facts of the attack and the human impact on the victims and their families. | Increased sensationalism and politicization of the attack, with some outlets focusing on the cultural and religious aspects of the story. | The competitive nature of the media landscape and the desire for exclusive or dramatic angles on the story. |
| Public Trust | Initial trust in the government and security agencies to handle the crisis and prevent future attacks. | Erosion of public trust in the government's ability to protect citizens and address the root causes of extremism. | Revelations about intelligence failures and the early release of the attacker from prison. |

Key Developments Influencing Public Opinion
1.	 Attacker's Background: As more details emerged about Kujtim Fejzulai's history of extremism, his attempts to join ISIS, and his early release from prison, public opinion shifted towards criticism of the government's counterterrorism policies and deradicalization programs.

2.	 Intelligence Failures: Reports that Slovak authorities had warned their Austrian counterparts about Fejzulai's attempt to purchase ammunition months before the attack led to growing concerns about intelligence-sharing mechanisms and inter-agency coordination.

3. Political Rhetoric: Statements by political leaders, particularly those from right-wing parties, emphasized the cultural and religious aspects of the attack, contributing to heightened debates about immigration, integration, and the role of Islam in Austrian society.

4. Media Coverage: The competitive nature of the media landscape led to increased sensationalism and politicization of the attack, with some outlets focusing on dramatic or divisive angles of the story.

Conclusion
In the week following the Vienna attack, public opinion in Austria underwent significant shifts as more information about the attack and its perpetrator emerged. Initial shock and fear gave way to growing concerns about security, immigration, and the role of Islam in Austrian society. The revelations about the attacker's background and the intelligence failures that allowed the attack to occur led to increased criticism of the government's counterterrorism policies and a erosion of public trust in the government's ability to protect citizens. The heightened debates and divisions that emerged in the wake of the attack underscored the complex and multifaceted nature of the challenges posed by terrorism and extremism.
