Officer's Account: Responding to the Vienna Attack
As a police officer on duty the night of November 2, 2020, I was one of the first responders to the terrorist attack in Vienna's city center. The events of that evening are etched into my memory, a stark reminder of the chaos and courage that unfolded in those critical minutes.
Initial Response
I was patrolling near Seitenstettengasse when the first reports of gunshots came over the radio. The dispatch was frantic, with multiple calls flooding in about an active shooter. My partner and I immediately activated our emergency lights and sped towards the scene, our adrenaline surging.
As we approached, the sound of gunfire grew louder, and the streets were filled with panicked civilians running for cover. We quickly assessed the situation and established a perimeter, directing bystanders to safety and away from the danger zone.
Engaging the Threat
Armed with our service weapons and protective gear, we moved cautiously towards the source of the gunfire. The attacker, later identified as Kujtim Fejzulai, was armed with an AK-47, a pistol, a machete, and what appeared to be an explosive vest. He was moving swiftly, firing indiscriminately at anyone in his path.
Our training kicked in, and we coordinated with other responding units to locate and neutralize the threat. Communication was key, and we relied on our radios to relay real-time information to our colleagues and command center.
Neutralizing the Attacker
Within inutes, we had Fejzulai in our sights. He was cornered near a café, still firing his weapon. We took cover behind a patrol car and returned fire, aiming to incapacitate him. The exchange was intense, with bullets flying in both directions. Despite the chaos, we maintained our composure and focused on our target.
After what felt like an eternity but was actually just a few minutes, Fejzulai was hit and fell to the ground. We approached cautiously, weapons drawn, and confirmed that he was no longer a threat. The entire ordeal, from the first gunshot to the neutralized attacker, lasted just nine minutes.
Securing the Scene
With the immediate threat eliminated, our focus shifted to securing the scene and tending to the victims. We called for backup and emergency medical services, knowing that every second counted. The streets were littered with debris, and the air was filled with the moans of the injured and the wails of the distressed.
We worked swiftly to establish a command post and coordinate the response efforts. Our priorities were to ensure the safety of the civilians, provide first aid to the wounded, and gather evidence for the investigation.
Assisting the Injured
As a trained first responder, I administered basic first aid to several victims while waiting for the paramedics to arrive. The injuries ranged from gunshot wounds to lacerations and blunt force trauma. The sight of so many innocent people suffering was heart-wrenching, but we remained focused on our duties.
Investigation and Aftermath
In the hours and days that followed, we worked tirelessly to piece together the events of that night. We interviewed witnesses, collected evidence, and collaborated with intelligence agencies to understand how the attack had been planned and executed.
The investigation revealed that Fejzulai had been known to authorities and had previously been convicted of attempting to join ISIS. Despite warnings from Slovak authorities about his attempt to purchase ammunition, the intelligence had not been acted upon effectively. This failure in communication and coordination was a stark reminder of the need for improved inter-agency collaboration.
Reflections
As a police officer, I am trained to respond to emergencies, but nothing could have fully prepared me for the horror of that night. The Vienna attack was a testament to the bravery and dedication of first responders, who risked their lives to protect others. It also highlighted the importance of vigilance, communication, and coordination in the face of such threats.
In the aftermath of the attack, we received an outpouring of support from the community, a reminder of the resilience and unity of the people of Vienna. We mourned the victims, honored the heroes, and vowed to learn from the failures that allowed the attack to occur.
As I continue to serve and protect, the memories of that night serve as a constant reminder of the sacrifices made by law enforcement officers and the importance of our mission. We stand ready to face whatever challenges may come, united in our commitment to keeping our city safe.
