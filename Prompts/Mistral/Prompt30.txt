Key Statements and Actions

1. Condemnation of the Attack:
	 The Islamic Religious Community in Austria (IGGÖ) issued a statement condemning the attack and expressing its condolences to the victims and their families.
	 "We are shocked and horrified by this cowardly act of violence. Our thoughts and prayers are with the victims and their families. We stand united with the people of Vienna and Austria in rejecting hatred and extremism." – IGGÖ President Ümit Vural.
2. Solidarity and Support:
	 Muslim community members participated in a candlelight vigil in Heldenplatz, one of Vienna's most iconic public squares, to honor the victims and express their solidarity.
	 "We are here tonight to stand together, to show that we are one community, united in our grief and our determination to overcome this tragedy." – A Muslim community leader who spoke at the vigil.
3. Fear and Anxiety:
	 Many Muslims in Austria reported experiencing heightened fear and anxiety in the wake of the attack, due to concerns about potential backlash and discrimination.
	 "I am afraid to go out, to wear my hijab, to be visible as a Muslim. I worry about what people might think, or what they might do." – A Muslim woman who shared her experiences with the media.
4. Community Engagement:
	 Muslim community leaders and organizations engaged in dialogue and cooperation with government officials, security agencies, and other community groups to address the root causes of extremism and promote social cohesion.
	 "We must work together, to build trust, to foster understanding, and to create a more inclusive and resilient society." – A Muslim community leader who participated in a roundtable discussion with government officials.
5. Educational Initiatives:
	 Muslim community organizations launched educational initiatives aimed at promoting a better understanding of Islam, its teachings, and its role in Austrian society.
	 "We want to share our story, our experiences, and our beliefs. We want to foster a more informed and nuanced public discourse about Islam and the Muslim community." – A Muslim community leader who helped to organize an educational event.
6. Support for Survivors:
	 Muslim community members offered support and assistance to the survivors of the attack and their families, regardless of their background or beliefs.
	 "We are here to help, to listen, and to stand together. We are all in this together, and we will get through it together." – A Muslim community leader who volunteered at a support center for survivors.
7. Advocacy and Activism:
	 Muslim community leaders and activists advocated for the rights and protections of the community, and worked to combat discrimination, Islamophobia, and other forms of bigotry.
	 "We must stand up for our rights, for our dignity, and for our place in this society. We must combat hatred and division, and promote understanding, tolerance, and inclusion." – A Muslim community activist who participated in a protest against Islamophobia.

Conclusion

In the aftermath of the Vienna attack, Austria's Muslim community responded with a mix of condemnation, solidarity, fear, and resilience. The community's swift and unequivocal rejection of the attack, its participation in vigils and memorials, and its engagement in dialogue and cooperation with other stakeholders demonstrated its commitment to peace, coexistence, and social cohesion. The experiences of fear and anxiety among many Muslims in Austria highlighted the need for greater support and protection for the community, and for efforts to combat Islamophobia and promote understanding and tolerance. The community's educational initiatives, support for survivors, and advocacy and activism reflected its determination to foster a more informed and nuanced public discourse about Islam and the Muslim community, and to promote a more inclusive and resilient society. As Vienna and the international community continued to grapple with the long-term impact of the attack, the responses and actions of Austria's Muslim community remained a powerful reminder of the strength, connection, and hope that can emerge in the face of tragedy and adversity.
