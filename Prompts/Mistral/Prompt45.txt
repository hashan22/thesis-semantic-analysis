Key Findings and Reflections

1. Attempt to Join ISIS:
	 Fejzulai's attempt to join ISIS in 2019 was a significant warning sign that was not adequately addressed by Austrian authorities, highlighting the need for continuous monitoring and support for individuals with a history of extremism, as well as comprehensive and evidence-based deradicalization programs.
2. Early Release from Prison:
	 Fejzulai's early release from prison, despite his history of radicalization and extremist ties, underscores the importance of rigorous and thorough assessment of offenders' true level of deradicalization and the potential risks they pose upon release.
3. Continued Extremist Ties:
	 Fejzulai's continued extremist ties after his release highlight the need for effective monitoring and disruption of extremist networks, as well as addressing the root causes of radicalization and promoting social cohesion and resilience.
4. Attempt to Purchase Ammunition:
	 The warning from Slovak authorities about Fejzulai's attempt to purchase ammunition months before the attack, which was not acted upon effectively, underscores the importance of improved intelligence sharing and coordination among security agencies, as well as the establishment of real-time data sharing platforms and regular joint exercises and training programs.
5. Online Radicalization:
	 Fejzulai's online radicalization, significantly influenced by extremist content and interactions with like-minded individuals, emphasizes the need for greater emphasis on understanding and countering online radicalization, including partnerships with tech companies to remove extremist content and educational programs on digital literacy and critical thinking.
6. Lack of Continuous Monitoring:
	 The lack of continuous monitoring and support for Fejzulai after his release underscores the importance of ongoing supervision and intervention for released offenders, as well as the need for comprehensive and evidence-based deradicalization programs that address the root causes of extremism and promote successful reintegration.
7. Inadequate Probation Conditions:
	 The inadequate probation conditions for Fejzulai highlight the need for stricter restrictions and supervision for individuals convicted of terrorism-related offenses, including regular check-ins with law enforcement and mandatory participation in deradicalization programs.

Conclusion

Several warning signs were missed or not adequately addressed before the Vienna attack, highlighting the need for continuous monitoring and support for individuals with a history of extremism, rigorous and thorough assessment of offenders' true level of deradicalization, effective monitoring and disruption of extremist networks, improved intelligence sharing and coordination among security agencies, greater emphasis on understanding and countering online radicalization, ongoing supervision and intervention for released offenders, and stricter probation conditions for individuals convicted of terrorism-related offenses. By learning from these missed warning signs and implementing the necessary reforms and improvements, Austria and the international community can work together to prevent similar incidents and build a more peaceful, secure, and inclusive society.
