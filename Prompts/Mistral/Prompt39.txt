Kujtim Fejzulai, the perpetrator of the Vienna attack on November 2, 2020, had a background marked by radicalization, extremist ties, and a history of criminal behavior. Here is an overview of his ideological and behavioral background:

| Aspect | Details |
|--------|---------|
| Early Life| Fejzulai was born in Vienna to parents of North Macedonian descent. He grew up in Austria and held dual Austrian and North Macedonian citizenship. |
| Radicalization| Fejzulai's radicalization process began in his teenage years, influenced by online extremist content and interactions with like-minded individuals. He adopted a radical interpretation of Islam, which justified violence and terrorism as a means to achieve political and religious goals. |
| Attempt to Join ISIS| In April 2019, Fejzulai attempted to travel to Syria to join the Islamic State (ISIS). He was arrested and subsequently convicted of terrorism-related offenses. |
| Conviction and Imprisonment| Fejzulai was sentenced to 22 months in prison for his attempt to join ISIS. During his imprisonment, he participated in deradicalization programs aimed at rehabilitating and reintegrating offenders. |
| Early Release| Despite his participation in deradicalization programs, Fejzulai was released early from prison in December 2019, after convincing authorities that he had been successfully deradicalized. |
| Continued Extremist Ties| Following his release, Fejzulai maintained contact with extremist networks and continued to consume and share extremist propaganda online. He also attempted to purchase ammunition, which raised concerns among security agencies. |
| Planning the Attack| In the months leading up to the attack, Fejzulai planned and prepared for the violent incident. He acquired weapons, including an assault rifle, a pistol, and a machete, and created a fake explosive belt to enhance the impact of his attack. |
| Execution of the Attack| On November 2, 2020, Fejzulai carried out the attack in Vienna's city center, targeting multiple locations and killing four people before being shot and killed by police. |

Key Findings and Context

1. Early Life and Radicalization:
	 Fejzulai's early life and radicalization process were influenced by a combination of personal, social, and ideological factors. His exposure to extremist content and interactions with like-minded individuals online played a significant role in his adoption of a radical interpretation of Islam.
2. Attempt to Join ISIS:
	 Fejzulai's attempt to join ISIS in 2019 demonstrated his commitment to the extremist cause and his willingness to engage in violent action to advance the group's goals. His arrest and conviction highlighted the challenges of detecting and preventing such attempts, as well as the need for effective monitoring and intervention.
3. Conviction, Imprisonment, and Early Release:
	 Fejzulai's conviction, imprisonment, and early release underscored the complexities and limitations of deradicalization programs, as well as the difficulties in assessing the true level of rehabilitation and the potential risks posed by released offenders.
4. Continued Extremist Ties and Planning the Attack:
	 Fejzulai's continued extremist ties and planning of the attack highlighted the challenges of effectively monitoring and supervising known extremists, as well as the need for comprehensive and evidence-based approaches to deradicalization and reintegration.
5. Execution of the Attack:
	 The execution of the attack demonstrated the devastating impact of lone wolf terrorism and the need for enhanced intelligence sharing, community engagement, and emergency response capabilities to prevent and respond to such incidents.

Conclusion

Kujtim Fejzulai's background in terms of ideology and past behavior was marked by radicalization, extremist ties, and a history of criminal behavior. His early life, radicalization process, attempt to join ISIS, conviction, imprisonment, early release, continued extremist ties, planning of the attack, and execution of the violent incident all contributed to the complex and multifaceted nature of his profile. Understanding his background and motivations is essential for developing effective counterterrorism strategies that address the root causes of extremism, promote social cohesion and resilience, and foster a more peaceful, secure, and inclusive society. By learning from the Vienna attack and its aftermath, Austria and the international community can work together to prevent similar incidents and build a brighter future for all.
