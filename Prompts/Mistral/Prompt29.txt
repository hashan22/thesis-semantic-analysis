In the days following the Vienna attack on November 2, 2020, survivors shared their harrowing experiences and emotional journeys with the media, offering vivid and poignant accounts of the event and its aftermath. Here are some of the ways survivors described the attack and its impact on their lives:

| Survivor | Description | Emotional Impact |
|----------|-------------|------------------|
| Witnesses| Described the initial confusion and chaos as the attack unfolded, with many initially mistaking the gunshots for fireworks or other noises. | Shock, disbelief, and a sense of surrealism, as well as feelings of fear, anxiety, and helplessness. |
| Those Who Fled| Recounted the panic and desperation as they ran for cover, seeking shelter in nearby buildings, shops, and restaurants. | Trauma, nightmares, and a heightened sense of vulnerability, as well as gratitude for the strangers who offered help and comfort. |
| The Injured| Shared their experiences of pain, fear, and uncertainty as they awaited medical assistance and transportation to hospitals. | Physical and emotional pain, as well as a sense of resilience and determination to recover and rebuild their lives. |
| First Responders| Described the professionalism, bravery, and swift action of the police, paramedics, and other emergency personnel who risked their lives to save others. | Pride in their work and the impact they had on the lives of others, as well as a sense of emotional toll and the need for support and self-care. |
| Community Members| Highlighted the outpouring of support, solidarity, and kindness from the Viennese community, as well as the importance of coming together to heal and rebuild. | A sense of connection, belonging, and hope, as well as a determination to stand together against hatred and violence. |

Key Themes and Quotes
1. Initial Confusion and Chaos:
	 "At first, I thought it was fireworks or something like that. But then I saw people running and screaming, and I realized something terrible was happening." – A witness who was near the scene of the attack.
	 "It was like a scene from a movie, but it was real. People were running in all directions, trying to find somewhere safe to hide." – A survivor who sought shelter in a nearby café.
2. Panic and Desperation:
	 "I just ran. I didn't know where I was going, but I knew I had to get away. I could hear the gunshots behind me, and I was terrified." – A survivor who fled the scene.
	 "I saw people helping each other, strangers offering shelter and comfort. It was like the best of humanity in the face of the worst." – A witness who observed acts of kindness and bravery.
3. Pain, Fear, and Uncertainty:
	 "I was lying on the ground, and I could feel the blood. I was so scared, but I tried to stay calm and wait for help." – A survivor who was injured in the attack.
	 "The paramedics were amazing. They were so calm and professional, and they saved my life. I will always be grateful to them." – A survivor who received medical assistance at the scene.
4. Professionalism, Bravery, and Swift Action:
	 "We knew we had to act fast. We trained for this, and we were ready. But nothing can really prepare you for the reality of it." – A first responder who was among the first to arrive at the scene.
	 "It was a team effort. Everyone worked together, and we were able to neutralize the threat and save lives." – A police officer who participated in the response.
5. Support, Solidarity, and Kindness:
	 "People brought food, water, and blankets. They offered to help in any way they could. It was like the whole city came together." – A community member who participated in the relief efforts.
	 "We are stronger together. That's what I learned from this. We have to stand together, no matter what." – A survivor who found comfort and hope in the outpouring of support from the community.

Conclusion
In the days following the Vienna attack, survivors shared their experiences and emotions with the media, offering vivid and poignant accounts of the event and its aftermath. The initial confusion and chaos, panic and desperation, pain, fear, and uncertainty, professionalism, bravery, and swift action, as well as support, solidarity, and kindness, were all key themes in their descriptions. These accounts highlighted the human impact of the tragedy, as well as the resilience, determination, and hope that emerged in its wake. As Vienna and the international community continued to grapple with the long-term effects of the attack, the voices and stories of the survivors remained a powerful reminder of the strength and connection of the human spirit.
