One month after the Vienna attack on November 2, 2020, the Austrian government introduced several new security measures aimed at enhancing counterterrorism capabilities, improving intelligence sharing, and addressing the root causes of extremism. Here are the key security measures implemented:

| Measure | Details |
|---------|---------|
| Indefinite Detention for Terror Offenders| Legislation allowing for the indefinite detention of individuals convicted of terror offenses, enabling authorities to keep high-risk offenders behind bars for life. |
| Electronic Surveillance of Released Offenders| Mandatory electronic surveillance and monitoring of individuals convicted of terrorism-related offenses upon their release from prison, to ensure compliance with probation conditions and detect signs of re-radicalization. |
| Enhanced Intelligence Sharing| Establishment of a new intelligence-sharing platform to facilitate real-time data exchange and analysis among security agencies, both domestically and internationally. |
| Stricter Probation Conditions| Tightened probation conditions for individuals convicted of terrorism-related offenses, including regular check-ins with law enforcement, restrictions on travel and communication, and mandatory participation in deradicalization programs. |
| Expanded Powers to Close Places of Worship| New powers for authorities to close places of worship deemed to promote extremism or radicalization, with a focus on preventing the spread of hateful ideologies. |
| Increased Police Presence| Deployment of additional police officers in public areas, particularly in city centers and other high-risk locations, to enhance visibility, deter potential attacks, and improve response times. |
| Enhanced Surveillance Capabilities| Investment in advanced surveillance technologies, such as facial recognition software and AI-powered analytics, to improve the detection and tracking of potential threats. |
| Strengthened Border Controls| Tightened border controls and enhanced screening procedures to prevent the entry of known extremists and detect suspicious activities. |
| Community Engagement Initiatives| Launch of community engagement programs aimed at fostering trust and cooperation between security agencies and local communities, with a focus on promoting social cohesion and resilience. |
| Counter-Radicalization Programs| Expansion of counter-radicalization programs, including educational initiatives, mentorship programs, and support networks, to address the root causes of extremism and prevent radicalization. |

Key Statements and Actions
1.	Chancellor Sebastian Kurz:
	 Announced the new security measures in a press conference on December 2, 2020, emphasizing the government's commitment to enhancing national security and preventing future attacks.
	 Stated, "We must focus much more strongly on the problem of political Islam in the future. I hope we will see an end to this misunderstood tolerance and that all countries in Europe will finally realize how dangerous the ideology of political Islam is for our freedom and the European way of life."
2.	Interior Minister Karl Nehammer:
	 Provided details on the implementation of the new security measures, including the establishment of the intelligence-sharing platform and the deployment of additional police officers.
	 Emphasized the importance of community engagement and cooperation in preventing radicalization and promoting social cohesion.
3.	Justice Minister Alma Zadić:
	 Outlined the legislative changes related to the indefinite detention of terror offenders and the electronic surveillance of released offenders.
	 Stated, "These measures are necessary to ensure the safety and security of our citizens and to prevent the re-offending of known extremists."

Conclusion
The new security measures introduced one month after the Vienna attack reflected the Austrian government's commitment to learning from the incident and enhancing its counterterrorism capabilities. The focus on intelligence sharing, community engagement, and addressing the root causes of extremism demonstrated a comprehensive and proactive approach to preventing future attacks. The coordinated efforts of government officials, security agencies, and community leaders aimed to restore a sense of safety and resilience in the wake of the tragedy.
