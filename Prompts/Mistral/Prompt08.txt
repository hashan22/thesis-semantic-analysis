Mayor's Address to the City of Vienna
November 3, 2020

Good morning, Vienna.
Last night, our beloved city was struck by a cowardly and senseless act of violence. As your mayor, I stand before you today with a heavy heart, but also with a deep sense of resolve and unity. We mourn the loss of innocent lives, and we stand in solidarity with the injured and their families. This attack was an assault on our city, our values, and our way of life. But let me be clear: Vienna will not be cowed by terrorism. We will not be divided by fear. We will stand together, united in our grief and our determination to overcome this tragedy.
Honoring the Victims
First and foremost, our thoughts and prayers are with the victims of this horrific attack and their families. We have lost precious lives—students, artists, workers, and dreamers—who were simply enjoying an evening in our beautiful city. Their loss is a profound tragedy for our community, and we will honor their memory by living our lives with the same passion and compassion they embodied.
We also stand with the injured and their families, offering our support and our commitment to their recovery. Our hospitals and medical professionals are providing the best possible care, and we will ensure that they have the resources and support they need during this difficult time.
Praising the Heroes
In the face of this attack, we have seen the best of Vienna. Our first responders—police officers, paramedics, and firefighters—acted with incredible bravery and professionalism, neutralizing the threat within minutes and saving countless lives. We are deeply grateful for their courage and dedication.
We have also witnessed the heroism of ordinary citizens who risked their own safety to help others. Their actions remind us of the strength and resilience of our community. In times of darkness, it is these acts of kindness and bravery that shine a light on our shared humanity.
Ensuring Safety and Security
I want to assure you that the city is taking every possible measure to ensure the safety and security of our citizens. We have increased police presence in public areas and are working closely with our national and international partners to monitor and address any potential threats.
An independent investigation has been launched to examine the circumstances surrounding the attack, including the intelligence and law enforcement responses. We are committed to learning from this tragedy and implementing the necessary reforms to prevent such incidents in the future.
Supporting Our Community
In the coming days and weeks, we will be providing support and resources to those affected by the attack. Our community centers, places of worship, and counseling services are open and available to anyone in need of assistance.
We also call on all Viennese to come together in solidarity, to support one another, and to reject the hatred and extremism that seeks to tear us apart. We are stronger together, and it is this unity that will see us through this difficult time.
Moving Forward
As we mourn the loss of innocent lives and tend to the injured, we must also look to the future. We will not let this attack define us or undermine our values. Vienna is a city of culture, diversity, and tolerance, and we will not allow terrorism to change who we are.
We will continue to celebrate our vibrant community, our rich history, and our shared humanity. We will stand together, united in our commitment to peace, hope, and resilience.
Conclusion
Last night, we witnessed the worst of humanity, but we also saw the best. We saw the courage of our first responders, the heroism of our citizens, and the strength of our community. It is this spirit that will guide us through the dark days ahead and inspire us to build a brighter future.
We honor the memory of those we lost. We stand with the injured and their families. And we reaffirm our commitment to the values that define us as a city—freedom, democracy, and the rule of law.
Vienna, we are strong. Vienna, we are united. Vienna, we will overcome.
Thank you, and may God bless our city and its people.
Michael Ludwig, Mayor of Vienna
