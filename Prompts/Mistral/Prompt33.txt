Key Shifts in International Reactions

1. From Condemnation to Cooperation:
	 In the immediate aftermath of the attack, international reactions focused on expressing condolences, solidarity, and support for Austria, as well as a united front against terrorism.
	 As the situation stabilized, the focus shifted towards enhanced collaboration among security agencies, intelligence sharing, and joint counterterrorism efforts to prevent further attacks.
2. From Solidarity to Scrutiny:
	 In the days following the attack, international reactions emphasized the sense of unity and connection among nations and communities in the face of terrorism.
	 As more information about the attacker's background and the intelligence failures that allowed the attack to occur emerged, international scrutiny of Austria's counterterrorism policies and practices increased.
3. From Sympathy to Strategy:
	 In the weeks following the attack, international reactions began to focus on the policy changes, legislative reforms, and long-term strategies needed to address the evolving terrorist threat.
	 Debates about immigration, integration, and the role of Islam in European societies, as well as discussions about the balance between security and civil liberties, became more prominent in international discourse.
4. From Reaction to Reflection:
	 In the months following the attack, international reactions shifted towards reflections on the attack's impact on international security, the evolution of the terrorist threat, and the need for a comprehensive and proactive approach to counterterrorism.
	 International forums, conferences, and publications focused on the lessons learned from the Vienna attack and their implications for global security, fostering a more thoughtful and informed public discourse.
